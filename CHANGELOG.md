# Changelog

All notable player-facing and packaging changes are documented here.

## 1.0.0 — Dawnveil launch build

Skybound Knight now includes the full endless-climb loop, persistent player profile, height-point armory, ten character styles, jump trails, landing effects, one-time record celebrations, and the revised Dawnveil Reverie interface.

The audio experience includes a procedural lo-fi acoustic score, independent Music and SFX controls, a high-altitude night arrangement, record cue, and responsive HUD equalizer. Mobile players have a left virtual joystick, right boost control, notch-aware layout spacing, and touch-safe navigation.

The project also includes a Capacitor Android wrapper with portrait orientation, lifecycle pausing, Android Back handling, optional wake lock, restrained haptics, and verified debug-APK packaging instructions.
